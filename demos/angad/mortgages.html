Common Questions

Mortgage FAQ

How much do I need for a down payment?

In Canada, minimum down payments are typically 5% on homes under $500,000, with higher tiers required above that. Homes over $1,500,000 generally require at least 20% down. A mortgage professional can confirm exact requirements for your situation.

What's the difference between pre-qualified and pre-approved?

Pre-qualification is a rough estimate based on self-reported numbers. Pre-approval involves your lender verifying documents and typically holding a rate for you, which is what sellers want to see with an offer.

Do I need mortgage default insurance?

If your down payment is less than 20% of the purchase price, your lender will typically require mortgage default (CMHC or private) insurance, added to your mortgage.

Should I choose a fixed or variable rate?

Fixed rates offer predictable payments for your term. Variable rates can move with the market and may save money long-term but carry more short-term uncertainty. The right choice depends on your comfort with risk and your timeline.
