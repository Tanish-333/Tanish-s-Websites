Our Standard

One Standard, Five Stores

Every store carries the same four departments: fresh produce, spices and lentils, sweets and snacks, and pooja essentials. Stock varies by branch and season, so call ahead for specific items.

Holiday & Operational Notice

All five stores are open daily, 10 AM to 9 PM. By provincial law, every branch is closed on Christmas Day. Hours on other statutory holidays (New Year's, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Labour Day, and Thanksgiving) can vary by branch, so please call ahead to confirm before visiting.
