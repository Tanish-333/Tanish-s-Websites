Our Story

A taste of Peru like no other

La Crema Peruvian Restaurant was born from a simple idea: share the food our family grew up on with the community we now call home. Every ceviche, every anticucho, every steaming plate of lomo saltado is made from scratch, using recipes handed down through generations.

We believe good food starts with good ingredients and honest technique. That’s why we source fresh fish for our ceviche, marinate our meats for hours, and slow-roast our chicken until it’s impossibly tender, because in Peruvian cooking, there are no shortcuts, only tradition.

Quality

Fresh ingredients, made-to-order, every single day.

Honest cooking, fair prices, and a genuine welcome.
