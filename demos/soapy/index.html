About Our Shop

Your neighborhood laundromat on Main Street East

Soapy Bubbles Laundromat is a neighborhood laundromat serving Hamilton locals. We keep our space clean and our machines running, so you can get your laundry done without hassle. Whether you're washing a single load or catching up on a full week, our self-serve machines are ready when you are. Need something handled for you? Our wash & fold and dry cleaning services take care of the rest. Stop by, start a load, and get on with your day.

Our Services

Everything your laundry needs

Self-Serve Laundry

A full lineup of washers and dryers for every load size. Choose a standard machine for everyday laundry, or use one of our large-capacity machines for bulky items like comforters, blankets, and pillows.

  • Standard & large-capacity washers
  • Fast, reliable dryers
  • Machines sized for comforters & blankets

Wash & Fold Drop-off

Short on time? Drop off your laundry and we'll take it from there. We wash, dry, and fold your clothes so they're ready for pickup. Just bring in your laundry, tell us your preferences, and pick up clean, folded clothes when it's convenient for you.

  • Drop off during business hours
  • Washed, dried & folded for you
  • Free pickup & delivery available

Dry Cleaning

For garments that need extra care, we offer dry cleaning drop-off right alongside our laundry services. Bring in suits, dresses, coats, and other delicate items and pick them up clean and ready to wear.

  • Drop-off dry cleaning service
  • Suits, dresses, coats & delicates
  • Ask in-store about pricing & turnaround
