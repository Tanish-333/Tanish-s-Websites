Our Story

Where creativity meets a little bit of magic

Expressive Unicorns started with a simple idea: face painting should feel like real art, not an afterthought. Every design is painted by hand with care, whether it's a first-timer's simple butterfly or an intricate glitter masterpiece.

We show up prepared, professional, and ready to bring color and joy to your event, from intimate birthday parties to citywide festivals. Beyond the brush, we also love creating handmade crafts and art for events and gifting.
