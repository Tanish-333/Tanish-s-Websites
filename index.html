FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Just email tanishdave333@gmail.com.

What kinds of businesses do you build websites for?

Local businesses and organizations of all kinds: restaurants, grocery stores, retail shops, lawn care and other trades, realtors, laundromats, community events, athletes and non-profits. You can see examples of each on this page.

Do you only work with businesses in Kitchener–Waterloo?

No. I'm based in Kitchener–Waterloo, Ontario, and I've built sites for clients in Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Hamilton, Toronto, Peel Region and beyond. Everything can be done by email, so location isn't a problem.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and features like online booking, ordering or an admin dashboard. Email me what you have in mind and I'll send you a clear quote before any work starts.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first, so it looks sharp and loads quickly on phones, tablets and laptops.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site is built with local SEO from day one: proper page titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap, fast loading and mobile-friendly pages. That gives your business the best chance of being found by people searching nearby.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. I can add simple admin tools so you can change menus, products, events and photos yourself without touching any code, and I'm available for updates whenever you need them.

How do I get started?

Email me at tanishdave333@gmail.com with a bit about your business and what you'd like your website to do. I'll reply with ideas and a quote.
